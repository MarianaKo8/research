This review summarizes recent progress in light-switchable proton, electron, and ionic conduction in framework materials. We discuss the mechanisms underlying the observed conduction changes, compare strategies for incorporating molecular photoswitches into MOFs, COFs, ZIFs, and related frameworks, and analyze the factors controlling their switching performance.
