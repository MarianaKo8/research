Here we introduce a Monte Carlo-based approach for predicting residual dipolar couplings in weakly aligned molecules. The comparison of different interaction models reveals the molecular contributions governing the alignment and shows that a simplified Coulomb model can provide better agreement with experiment than explicit-solvent MD for several molecules. The results further demonstrate the importance of molecular conformational flexibility for accurate RDC predictions.
