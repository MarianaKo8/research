We demonstrate and explain pronounced differences in two-photon absorption among four MOFs constructed from the same pyrene-based linker, with the cross sections following SrTBAPy < NU-1000 < NU-901 < BaTBAPy and reaching 8.2 × 10<sup>4</sup> GM for BaTBAPy. Quantum mechanical calculations reveal that these differences originate from changes in linker orientation, metal coordination environments, intermolecular coupling, and charge-transfer characteristics. These insights into the structure-property relationships of MOFs pave the way for rational design of next-generation nonlinear optical materials.
